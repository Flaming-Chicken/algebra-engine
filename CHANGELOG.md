# Changelog

All notable changes to `algebra-engine` (URAE) are documented in this file.
Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and Semantic Versioning.

## [Unreleased]

### Added and fixed

- Added: exact list utilities `sum`, `cumsum`, `cumprod`, `diff([...])`, `sort`, `reverse`, `len`, `unique`, `count`, `argmax`, `argmin`, `min([...])`, `range(n)`, `linspace`, `arange`, `sum_squares`, `geometric_mean`, `harmonic_mean`, `rms`, `weighted_mean`, `moving_average`, `rank_data`, `histogram`. `range(5)` is now `[0, 1, 2, 3, 4]` (it printed the statistical range, 0).
- Fixed: `solve(x^8 = 1)` left `RootOf` entries and `solve(x^6 = 1)` printed nested radicals; binomial equations `a x^n + b = 0` now give exact roots for n = 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24 (and 3, 4, 6, 12 for a negative right-hand side), for example `1/2 · i · √(3) + 1/2` for `x^6 = 1`.
- Added: percentages (`15% of 80`, `80 + 15%`, `200 - 10%`, `15%`), `0.75 as fraction`, and exact `round(x, n)` to n decimal places (ties away from zero, so `round(2.675, 2)` is `2.68`; negative n rounds to tens, hundreds, ...).
- Added: finite graphs. `spanning_trees(K4)`, `chromatic_polynomial(C5)`, `chromatic_number`, `degree_sequence`, `is_connected`, `components`, `is_bipartite`, `is_tree`, `shortest_path(G, a, b)` and `diameter`, on named graphs (`K4`, `C5`, `P4`, `S4`) or edge lists (`[[1,2],[2,3]]`).
- Added: degrees as input (`sin(30°)`, `cos(60 degrees)`, `45 deg`), `degrees`/`radians`/`rad2deg`/`deg2rad`, `celsius_to_fahrenheit` and the other temperature conversions. Fixed: a negated denominator in a solution (`-1 / (-(1/a + 1/b))`) now prints with the sign moved to the numerator.
- Added: propositional-logic commands `truth_table(F)`, `tautology(F)`, `satisfiable(F)`, `contradiction(F)`, `equivalent(F, G)`, `count_models(F)`; set notation `|{1,2,3}|`, `cardinality({...})`, `{1,2} x {3,4}` and `cartesian_product(A, B)`.
- Fixed: `5 xor 3` printed `15 · xor`, `0xFF` printed 0, `0b1010 + 1` printed 1 and `powerset({1,2})` was garbled. Added `0x`/`0b`/`0o` literals, `&`, `|`, `xor`, `<<`, `>>` on integers, `255 to binary` / `in hex` / `as octal`, and the spellings `bin`, `to_binary`, `to_hex`, `from_binary`, `from_hex`, `to_octal`.
- Added: `arithmetic_sum(a, d, n)`, `geometric_sum(a, r, n)`, `hypotenuse(a, b)`, `area_triangle` (Heron's formula or three points), `angle_between(u, v)` (exact multiples of pi) and `maclaurin(f, n)`; `maclaurin(cos(x), 6)` used to return `cos(x)` unchanged.
- Added: exact `binomial_cdf`, `hypergeometric_pmf`, `z_score`, `bayes(prior, sensitivity, false_positive)`, `expected_value(values, probabilities)` and `variance_discrete`. They were echoed back.
- Fixed: `10!!` was read as the factorial of 10! (a wrong answer); `n!!` is now the double factorial. Added `primes(n)` (also `primes_up_to`), `digit_sum` (also `sum_of_digits`) and `is_square`.
- Fixed: `2 x`, `a x + b`, `x y` and `solve(a x^2 + b x + c = 0, x)` (a space between factors is a product, tried when the input does not parse otherwise); solutions print square roots as `√(2)` instead of `2^(1/2)`.
- Fixed: `convert 3 kg + 500 g to g` dropped the second quantity (it printed 3000 g); sums and differences of quantities now convert correctly, also written as a sentence (`3 kg + 500 g to g`, `5 km to m`). Added `10 choose 3` and `|x|`. The command-line program no longer treats a line starting with `[` or `{` as JSON unless it is JSON, so bracket matrices and set literals print normally.
- Added: calculus the way it is written: `d/dx x^3`, `d^2/dx^2 f`, `∂/∂x f`, `lim x->0 f`, `∫ x^2 dx`, `∫_0^1 x^2 dx`, `∑_{k=1}^{4} k`, `sqrt 16`, `sin x`, `nth_root(27, 3)`; `derivative(f, x, 2)` now gives the second derivative.
- Fixed: a constant typed with a decimal (`sin(0.5)`, `ln(2.0)`, `normal_cdf(1.96)`) now also prints its floating-point value after the exact form (`sin(1/2) ≈ 0.479425538604203`).
- Added: `fit`, `polyfit` and `linear_regression` take two lists (`fit([1,2,3],[2,4,6])`); `lcm` of polynomials; `subs`, `substitute`, `replace`, `evaluate` and `eval` substitution (`subs(x^2 + y, x = 2, y = 3)`).
- Added: `nCr`, `nPr`, `prime_pi`, `tribonacci`, `compositions(n)` and `compositions(n, k)`; discrete function names are case-insensitive. They were echoed back unevaluated.
- Added: constrained extrema by Lagrange multipliers (`maximize x*y subject to x + y = 10`, `minimize x^2 + y^2 subject to 3*x + 4*y = 10`), classified as local maximum or minimum; `extrema` and `critical_points` now work when the derivative cannot be factored exactly (`x^2*exp(-x)`).
- Added: `rk4`, `euler_method`, `heun` and `midpoint_method` for `y' = f(x, y)` with a chosen number of steps.
- Added: `residue(f, x, a)` (real or complex point; exact rationals when recognised), `poles(f)` with orders, and `abs` of a complex constant (`abs(1+i)` is `√(2)`).
- Added: `arg`, `modulus`, `polar`, `atan2` (exact multiples of pi), `convolve`, and `dft`/`fft`/`ifft` on constant lists. They were echoed back unevaluated.
- Added: `critical_points(f)` and `extrema(f)` (exact stationary points, classified as local minimum or maximum), `simpson`, `trapezoid` and `midpoint` quadrature with `n` subintervals, and the root-finder spellings `newton_method`, `bisection`, `secant`. They were echoed back unevaluated.
- Added: exact linear programming, `maximize 3x + 2y subject to x + y <= 4, x + 3y <= 6` (also `minimize`, `max`, `min`, `s.t.`, constraints with `<=`, `>=`, `=`; non-negative variables): a two-phase simplex method with Bland's rule over the rationals gives the optimal value and a vertex exactly (`minimum value = 14/5 at x = 8/5, y = 6/5`), or reports `infeasible` or `unbounded`. These were parse errors.
- Added: `next_term 1, 4, 9, 16`, `find_pattern 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8`, `nth_term 2, 4, 6, 8`: the rule of a sequence from its first terms, and the next five terms. A polynomial in `n` is tried first (finite differences, with one confirming term), then a linear recurrence with constant coefficients of order at most 4 (Berlekamp-Massey over the rationals, one confirming term), with its closed form from `rsolve`; nothing is invented when no rule fits (the primes). Terms are numbered from 1.
- Added: closed forms of linear recurrences with constant coefficients, `rsolve a(n+2) = a(n+1) + a(n), a(0) = 0, a(1) = 1` is `sqrt(5)/5 ((1 + sqrt 5)/2)^n - sqrt(5)/5 ((1 - sqrt 5)/2)^n` (also `solve_recurrence`, `recurrence`): rational roots with multiplicities (`2^n n` for a double root), a quadratic factor with irrational or complex roots, forcing terms `P(n) b^n` by undetermined coefficients with the resonance factor `n^m`, any shift (`a(n) = a(n-1) + a(n-2)`), order up to 6; initial values at any indices. The closed form is checked against the recurrence on eight terms before it is returned; they were parse errors.
- Added: series at infinity, `series(1/(x+1), x, oo, 4)` is `x^(-1) - x^(-2) + x^(-3) - x^(-4)` (an expansion in `1/x`: substitute `x = 1/t`, clear the inner fractions, expand about `t = 0`, substitute back), for rational functions, `exp(1/x)`, `sin(1/x)`, `ln(1 + 1/x)` and similar; it was an error.
- Fixed: `e^(i*pi)` was printed unevaluated while `exp(i*pi)` was `-1`; `e^x` is now `exp(x)` in the simplifier, so every rule for `exp` applies to both spellings (`e^(i*pi/2)` is `i`, `e^(ln 2)` is `2`, `diff(e^(x^2), x)` prints `2 x exp(x^2)` instead of the ambiguous `2 e^x^2 x`). The limit `(1 + 2/x)^x` at infinity prints `exp(2)`. A sum, product or limit written in LaTeX applies to the next term only (`\sum_{k=1}^{4} k + 1` is 11, not 14), and `sum(...)` and `product(...)` calls work inside arithmetic (`sum(k, k, 1, 4) + 1`, `2*sum(k, k, 1, 4)`).
- Fixed: LaTeX input. `\int_0^1 x^2 dx` was a parse error (the `\in` of `\int` was read as the membership sign), `\sum`, `\prod`, `\lim`, `\sqrt[3]{27}`, `\frac{d}{dx} f`, `\binom{n}{k}`, `\log_2 8`, `\ln e`, `\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x`, `\pi r^2` and `\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}` were parse errors, and `e^{i\pi}` was read as the backslash operator (`mldivide`). LaTeX is now rewritten into the engine's syntax first: integrals with bounds, sums, products, limits (also one-sided `0^+`), derivatives of any order, roots, binomials, logarithms with a base, function powers, Greek letters with implicit multiplication, and matrix environments.
- Added: plain English: `what is 2+2`, `calculate 3*4`, `2 plus 3`, `10 divided by 4`, `3 squared`, `2 to the power 10`, `square root of 16`, `factorial of 5`, `absolute value of -3`, `20 percent of 150`, `sine of 0`; number words `one` to `ten`; the case of symbols is kept.
- Added: continuous distributions in `f64` (accuracy about 1e-14): the regularised incomplete gamma and beta functions (`gamma_inc_p`, `gamma_inc_q`, `beta_inc`), `chi2_cdf`, `chi2_pdf`, `chi2_quantile`, `gamma_cdf`, `gamma_pdf`, `beta_cdf`, `beta_pdf`, `t_cdf`, `t_pdf`, `t_quantile`, `f_cdf`, `normal_quantile` (with an optional mean and deviation); they print their value as plain input and work inside `N(...)`. They were echoed back.
- Added: at any precision, `zeta(s)` for every real `s != 1` (it was `s > 1` only: `N(zeta(1/2), 40)`, `zeta(-1/2)`, `zeta(-3/2)`), the Hurwitz zeta function `hurwitz_zeta(s, a)` (also `zeta(s, a)`), `polygamma(m, x)` for `x > 0` and `besseli(n, x)` (the modified Bessel function of integer order). The `f64` values of these are rounded from the high-precision ones. Checked against published digits, the functional equation of the Riemann zeta function at three non-integer points to 40 digits, the polygamma recurrence, the Hurwitz relation, `psi'(1) = pi^2/6` and the Bessel recurrence.
- Added: `kron(A, B)` (Kronecker product), `diagonalize(A)` (`P` and `D` with `A = P D P^-1`, checked exactly before it is printed; a defective matrix is an error) and `svd(A)` / `singular_values(A)` (the singular values, exact, largest first). Matrix functions called with an unusable argument report the reason instead of echoing the input.
- Changed: a plain expression with symbols is shown in its simplified form when that is not longer than the expression as typed (it was: strictly shorter), so `x + x` is `2 · x`, `1*x` is `x`, `sin(-x)` is `-sin(x)`, `tan(-x)` is `-tan(x)`. Added simplifier rules: `abs(-x) = abs(x)` and `exp(a) * exp(b) = exp(a + b)` (so `exp(x) * exp(-x)` is `1`).
- Added: statistics `percentile [data], p`, `quantile [data], q` (linear interpolation, the usual type-7 definition), `quartiles`, `iqr`, `skewness`, `kurtosis` (excess) and `zscore`; and plane and space geometry on points written as lists or tuples: `distance((0,0),(3,4))`, `midpoint`, `slope`, `angle([1,0],[0,1])`, `triangle_area((0,0),(4,0),(0,3))`; all were parse errors or echoed.
- Added: closed forms of infinite sums: telescoping rational functions with integer poles through partial fractions and harmonic numbers (`sum(1/(k*(k+1)), k, 1, oo) = 1`, `1/(k*(k+2))` is `3/4`, `1/(k^2-1)` from 2 is `3/4`, `1/(k (k+1) (k+2))` is `1/4`), `sum(x^k/factorial(k), k, 0, oo) = exp(x)`, `sum(x^k/k, k, 1, oo) = -ln(1 - x)`, and the alternating harmonic, Leibniz and `1/(2k-1)^2` series; they gave `Symbolic sum closed form unavailable`.
- Added: named formulas of probability and geometry, exact and numerical (`N(...)`): `normal_pdf`, `normal_cdf` (with an optional mean and deviation), `exponential_pdf`, `exponential_cdf`, `poisson_pmf`, `binomial_pmf`, `geometric_pmf`, `uniform_cdf`, `circle_area`, `circle_circumference`, `sphere_volume`, `sphere_area`, `cylinder_volume`, `cone_volume`, `hypot`, `deg2rad`, `rad2deg`; they were echoed back (and `N(normal_cdf(1.96))` was an error).
- Added: `dec2bin`, `bin2dec`, `dec2hex` (`hex`), `hex2dec`, `dec2oct`, `oct2dec`; `subsets` (power set), `permutations_list`, `cycle_type` and `next_permutation` of a list (numbers or symbols); `pell(d)` (the fundamental solution of `x^2 - d y^2 = 1` by the continued fraction of `sqrt(d)`, exact for `d = 61` and beyond) and `diophantine(a*x + b*y = c)` (the general integer solution, or the reason there is none). All were echoed back unevaluated.
- Fixed: matrix arithmetic typed as plain input was echoed back as `matrix(row(...)) + matrix(row(...))`: `A + B`, `A - B`, `2*A`, `A/2`, `A * B`, `A * v`, `v * A`, `A^3`, `A^-1`, `x*A`, `transpose(A) * A`, `det(inverse(A))`, `identity(3)`, `zeros(2,3)`, `ones`, `diag([..])`, `trace`, `norm`, `charpoly`, `eigenvalues`, `eigenvectors`, `expm`, `cholesky`, `qr`, `solve_linear(A, b)` evaluate with exact (and symbolic) entries; the eigenvalues of a rotation are `i, -i` instead of `(-4)^(1/2) / 2`. The Frobenius norm of a matrix was `sqrt(matrix(row(1, 2))^2 + ...)`.
- Added: `carmichael(n)`, `order_mod(a, n)`, `sqrt_mod(a, p)` (Tonelli-Shanks), `discrete_log(g, h, p)` (baby-step giant-step), `radical(n)`, `gray_code(n)`.
- Fixed: a command inside the arguments of another command was not evaluated: `factor(expand(P))` printed `expand(...)` unevaluated, `integrate(expand((x+1)^3), x)` and `solve(expand((x-1)*(x-2)) = 0, x)` failed. Inner `expand`, `simplify`, `cancel`, `together`, `factor`, `diff`, `apart`, `trigsimp`, `expand_trig` and `collect` calls are now evaluated first and replaced by their results. `expand((x+1)^60)` was echoed back (the exponent limit was 32): powers expand while the number of terms of the result stays below 40,000.
- Added: logic and set expressions as plain input. `a and not a` is reported as a contradiction, `a or not a` as a tautology, `p implies q` as contingent with a satisfying and a refuting assignment (`and`, `or`, `not`, `xor`, `implies`, `iff`, `->`, `<->`, `&`, `|`, `~`); they were parse errors. `{1,2,3} union {3,4}`, `intersect`, `minus`, `xor`, `subset`, `{1,2} cross {a,b}`, `size {..}` and `2 in {1, 2}` (which was read as the domain declaration `2 in Reals`, printing `∀ 2 ∈ Reals`) evaluate on finite sets; equal rationals are one element.
- Fixed: `convert 5 km to miles` answered `5` and `convert 100 F to C` answered `100` (every pair that was not km/h and m/s was the identity). Unit conversion is now dimensional analysis: seven SI base dimensions, SI prefixes, derived and customary units (`N`, `Pa`, `J`, `kWh`, `atm`, `psi`, `mph`, `ft`, `mi`, `lb`, `eV`, ...), compound units (`m/s^2`, `kg*m/s^2`), the affine temperature scales, and an error for incompatible dimensions (`convert 5 km to kg`) or an unknown unit.
- Fixed: `0/0` was `1` and `oo - oo` was `0` (the rules `x/x = 1` and `x - x = 0` applied to values that are not finite non-zero numbers); `0/0`, `1/0`, `5/(3-3)`, `0^(-1)`, `oo - oo`, `sin(1/0)` are `Undefined`.
- Added: exact `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `cot`, `sec`, `csc` at every multiple of `pi/12` (`sin(pi/12) = (sqrt(6) - sqrt(2))/4`, `tan(pi/12) = 2 - sqrt(3)`, `cos(7 pi/12)`); the inverse of a sum of square roots by repeated conjugation. Added: statistics `mode`, `range`, `covariance` and `correlation` (a list of pairs or two lists; exact, with the decimal value).
- Added: exact arithmetic with radicals and logarithms of rationals, for constant expressions. `2^(3/2)` is `2 sqrt(2)`, `8^(1/3)` is `2`, `27^(2/3)` is `9`, `(sqrt(2) + 1)^2` is `3 + 2 sqrt(2)`, `1/sqrt(2)` is `sqrt(2)/2`, `1/(1 + sqrt(2))` is `sqrt(2) - 1`, `cbrt(16)` is `2 * 2^(1/3)`, `root(32, 5)` is `2`; `exp(2 ln 3)` is `9`, `e^(ln 2 + ln 3)` is `6`, `exp(ln(2)/2)` is `sqrt(2)`, `ln(8)/ln(2)` is `3`, `log2(1/8)` is `-3`, `log10(1000)`, `log(81, 3)`, `ln(27)/ln(9)` is `3/2`. They were echoed back as typed. Definite integrals whose value is a combination of roots are printed in that form (`integrate(sqrt(x), x, 1, 2)` is `4/3 sqrt(2) - 2/3`, not `2/3 2^(3/2) - 2/3 1^(3/2)`).
- Changed: a plain expression with symbols (`x^2*x^3`, `x^5/x^2`, `(x^3)^2`) is shown in its simplified form when that is strictly shorter than the expression as typed (`x^5`, `x^3`, `x^6`); it was echoed unchanged.
- Fixed: `solve(x^2 > 4, x)` returned `[2, -2]` and `solve(abs(x) < 3, x)` returned `[3, -3]`: an inequality was solved as an equation. Inequalities in one variable now give a union of intervals (`(-oo, -2) U (2, oo)`, `[0, 4)` for `sqrt(x) < 2`, `(-1, 0) U (1, oo)`, `{1}` for `(x-1)^2 <= 0`), with the zeros, the poles and the domain boundaries of `sqrt`, `ln` and rational powers as critical points; periodic functions (`sin(x) > 0`) are an error.
- Fixed: limits. `limit(abs(x)/x, x, 0, +)` returned `0` (the value is `1`), `limit(exp(1/x), x, 0, +)` returned `exp(1/0)`, `limit(sin(1/x), x, 0)` returned `sin(1/0)`, `limit(tan(x), x, pi/2)` returned `tan(pi/2)`, and the fourth argument of `limit(f, x, a, +)` was dropped. The call spelling now takes a side (`+`, `-`, `right`, `left`), and every limit at a finite point is checked against samples at `a +- h`: when the symbolic answer contradicts a clear numerical behaviour (a finite value, divergence, or two different one-sided limits) the numerical answer replaces it, labelled a numerical estimate; an answer that still contains a division by zero is `Undefined`.
- Fixed: `integrate(exp(-x^2), x, -oo, oo)` returned the rational `582540/328663` (an approximation of `sqrt(pi)` presented as exact); Gaussian integrals `c exp(a x^2 + b x + d)` over the line or the half line are now exact (`sqrt(pi)`, `sqrt(pi)/2`, `exp(1) sqrt(pi)`). A definite integral whose value is an exact rational is folded (`integrate(sqrt(x), x, 0, 4)` is `16/3`, not `2/3 4^(3/2) - 2/3 0^(3/2)`).
- Fixed: Laurent series. `series(1/x, x, 0, 3)` returned `x^2/2 + x^3/6`, `series(1/(x*(1-x)), x, 0, 3)` contained `oo`, `series(exp(x)/x^3, x, 0, 4)` ended in `+ oo`, and `series(1/x + 1/(1-x), x, 0, 3)`, `series(cot(x), x, 0, 5)` and `series(x*cot(x), x, 0, 4)` were reported as not analytic. A function with a pole at the point now gets its exact Laurent expansion from power-series arithmetic (`x^(-1)`, `1/x + 1 + x + x^2 + ...`, `cot x = 1/x - x/3 - x^3/45 - 2 x^5/945`, `1/(e^x - 1)` with the Bernoulli numbers, `1/sin x`), and removable singularities (`x cot x`, `x/(e^x - 1)`) are cancelled in the division. `cot`, `csc` and `sec` are in the fast family. The oracle is the function itself a short distance from the point.
- Added: Cartesian vector calculus: `gradient(f, [x, y])`, `hessian`, `jacobian([f1, f2], [x, y])`, `divergence`, `curl` (3-D and 2-D), `laplacian`, and `cross`, `dot`, `norm` of lists; they were echoed back unevaluated. Checked by `curl(grad f) = 0`, `div(curl F) = 0`, `laplacian = div(grad)`, central finite differences, and Lagrange's identity for the cross product.
- Added: initial value problems, `dsolve(y' = 2*y, y(0) = 1)`, `dsolve(y'' + y = 0, y(0) = 1, y'(0) = 0)`: the constants of the general solution are fixed from the conditions (exactly, by elimination over the rationals; a single nonlinear condition such as `y' = y^2` goes to the equation solver), and too few conditions is an error. They were a parse error. Added: trigonometric expansion `expand(sin(2*x))`, `expand(cos(x + y))`, `expand_trig(tan(x + y))` (angle addition and integer multiples) and `trigsimp` (the simplifier, the expansion and the reduction through `sin^2 = 1 - cos^2`; the shortest form wins: `trigsimp(sin(x)^4 - cos(x)^4)` is `1 - 2 cos(x)^2`). Fixed: `dsolve y' = 2*y` printed `exp(--2 x)`; `-(c f)` with a negative integer coefficient `c` is built as `(-c) f`.
- Added: matrix powers `[[1,2],[3,4]]^3`, `matpow(M, n)` (square matrices, negative powers through the inverse, symbolic entries) and `lu(M)`; these were echoed back as `matrix(row(...))^3`. `factor(2^32+1)`, `factor(10!)` and `factor(2^64+1)` give the prime factorisation of a constant integer expression (they were echoed as the evaluated number).
- Fixed: constant expressions that mention `pi` or `e` were echoed back (`sin(pi/6)`, `cos(pi/3)`, `tan(pi/4)`, `sin(pi/4)`); they are simplified now (`1/2`, `1/2`, `1`, `sqrt(2)/2`). Added: exact complex arithmetic on constant expressions (`(1+2i)*(3-i)` is `5 + 5i`, `(2+3i)/(1-i)`, `(1+i)^8 = 16`, `sqrt(-4) = 2i`, `abs(3+4i) = 5`, `conj`, `re`, `im`); these were echoed back unevaluated.
- Fixed: `series(x^x^x, x, 0, 6)` returned `0` (the derivative method read `0^0` as 1); a power whose base is zero at the expansion point and whose exponent depends on the variable (`x^x`, `sin(x)^x` at 0, `(x - 1)^x` at 1) is now reported as not analytic there.
- Added: arbitrary-precision `digamma`, `zeta(s)` for real `s > 1` (Euler-Maclaurin), `beta(a, b)` and `besselj(n, x)` for integer order (`N(zeta(3), 45)`, `N(digamma(1), 45)`, `N(besselj(0, 10), 30)`), checked against the published digits of Apery's constant, zeta(5) and the Euler-Mascheroni constant, against the recurrence and reflection identities of psi, `B(2, 3) = 1/12`, `B(1/2, 1/2) = pi` and the Bessel recurrence. The `f64` values of `zeta` and `digamma` are rounded once from these (`zeta(3)` was off in the last digit).
- Fixed: the simplifier treated the plain symbols `j` and `k` as quaternion units, so `k^2` became `-1`: `simplify(exp(-k^2/4))` returned `exp(1/4)`, `fourier(exp(-x^2), x, k)` returned `exp(1/4) sqrt(pi)` (no `k` at all) and any sum or product over an index `k` or `j` was at risk. They are ordinary variables now (`i` is still the imaginary unit); the quaternion units are `qi`, `qj`, `qk`.
- Fixed: the call spellings `expand((x+1)^5)`, `factor(x^4 - 1)`, `gcd(x^2 - 1, x^3 - 1)`, `cancel`, `collect`, `together`, `horner`, `discriminant`, `resultant`, `roots`, `laplace`, `fourier`, `ilt` were echoed back unevaluated; they mean the same as the command spellings. Added: `product(f, k, a, b)` (exact finite products, and closed forms for `c`, `k`, `c^k` with a symbolic upper limit) and `ztransform(x[n], n, z)` for sums of terms `c n^m a^n`.
- Added: list-valued and Euclidean integer functions: `divmod(a, b)`, `a // b` (floor division, exact for rationals), `digits(n, base)`, `egcd(a, b)` (with the Bezout coefficients), `crt([r...], [m...])` (moduli need not be coprime; an inconsistent system is an error), `continued_fraction(p/q)` and `convergents(p/q)`. - `UniversalBridge::weyl_to_diff_polynomial` returns `Result` and `DiffPolynomial` to `WeylDOperator` is now `TryFrom`: conversions that would drop an `x` power, flatten a nonlinear term or merge indeterminates are errors instead of silently wrong output.
- Differential-equation classification reports PDEs as linear, semilinear, quasilinear or nonlinear by the location of the nonlinearity (the KdV equation is now semilinear, Burgers quasilinear). Powers and products of derivatives are detected as nonlinear.
- Production code no longer contains reachable `unwrap`, `expect` or `panic!` calls outside documented operator panics; the workspace lints `clippy::unwrap_used`, `expect_used` and `panic` are on (test code exempt). A failed notebook launch is reported instead of panicking, and a poisoned token lock is recovered.
- `KnotInvariants::kauffman_bracket` and `jones_polynomial` return `Result` and compute a state sum (previously a lookup table for three knots and a product of `A^±1` otherwise). A positive `σ₁³` now gives `A⁻⁷ − A⁻³ − A⁵`, the standard right-handed trefoil (the old table held its mirror image).
- `AnytimeHandle::refine_step` and `run_to_budget` take a re-evaluation closure and `refine_with` accepts only sound (contained, tighter) balls; enclosures no longer shrink by a fixed factor.
- Planning consolidated: `docs/PLANNING.md` indexes the planning documents and tracks every known stub and defect against the code; `ROADMAP.md` marks phases 12-16, 22, 23 and 27 as Partial and lists the scope that has no implementation.
- `pade!` returns `AlgebraResult<ExprId>` and computes a real approximant (it returned a placeholder quotient).
- `creative_telescoping_integral`, `EmpiricalDistributionMut::{pdf, cdf}` and an unreachable AI provider return errors instead of a fixed Gaussian ODE, constants and a canned `f(x)` explanation.
- `puiseux_series` with a fractional exponent, `log`, `preset` and AI queries in the executor return errors instead of placeholder output.
- Unsupported summands, complex-root and higher-order recurrences return an error instead of a wrong formula.

### Fixed
- `poly::groebner_basis` returned its input with zero polynomials removed; it now computes the reduced Groebner basis over the rationals. `SparseMonomial::cmp_grevlex` is a genuine graded reverse lexicographic order.
- `Mesh3D::extrude_profile` capped non-convex outlines with a fan from one vertex (overlapping or inverted faces); it now ear-clips the polygon, handles either winding, and rejects degenerate or self-intersecting profiles.
- `NacaAirfoil::generate_3d_mesh` and `HelicalSpring::generate_3d_mesh` returned open shells without end caps (so `compute_volume` was meaningless); they are now closed solids. The airfoil outline no longer repeats its leading-edge point. `ThreadedScrew` meshes are documented as open surfaces.
- `GpuComputeKernel::enabled` was ignored; it now selects the parallel or sequential path. The `algebra_advanced::gpu` documentation no longer claims SIMD or GPU offload (the kernels are Rayon-parallel CPU code).
- `EGraph::extract_min_cost` costed only the chosen node's operator, not its operands, so the cheapest-looking node was not the cheapest expression; it now returns the total cost of the cheapest expression, and handles cyclic classes.
- `InvoluteGear::generate_3d_mesh` ignored `bore_radius` and capped the ends with a fan from one vertex of the non-convex tooth outline, giving overlapping or inverted faces. It is now a closed solid with a real bore and annular end caps (volume equals the outline area minus the bore, times the face width).
- `GrobnerProver` (lemma mining) reduced the difference of two expressions modulo an empty ideal, so it could not use identities such as `sin^2 + cos^2 = 1`. It now extracts the side relations of the transcendental atoms present and decides membership exactly over the rationals.
- `GeometricConstraintSolver::solve` ignored every constraint except distance and coincidence (placing a point along the x-axis); it now solves parallel, perpendicular, angle, tangent and fixed-area constraints too by damped Gauss-Newton, and reports inconsistent systems.
- `IgaStiffnessMatrix::integrate_stiffness_entry_2d` ignored the NURBS basis and returned a fixed weight of 1.0 on the diagonal and 0.1 elsewhere times the area; it now integrates the real gradient inner product (the unit square gives the textbook Q1 matrix). `metric_determinant_2d` uses analytic tangents.
- `DiffPolynomial::differentiate` left repeated indeterminates in a term (`y'` twice), which made degrees and leaders wrong; it now normalises.
- LSP hover evaluated only the hovered line, so any line using an earlier definition showed an error or a wrong value; it now evaluates the document up to that line in order.
- `StateSpaceSystem::is_stable` judged systems with three or more states by the sign of the trace; it now applies Routh-Hurwitz (continuous) or Schur-Cohn (discrete) to the characteristic polynomial.
- `frequency_response` returned zeros for every system that was not a two-state SISO one, and ignored the sample time of discrete systems; it now evaluates `C(sI - A)^-1 B + D` in general.
- `pole_placement_ackermann` supports any controllable single-input system (it stopped at two states).
- `UnificationEngine::unify` only handled a top-level symbol or number and failed on everything else. It is now a structural first-order unifier with an occurs check; `SubstitutionMap::apply` applies the result.
- The CLI proof export gated its step on unifying an expression with its own simplification, which almost never succeeded and is not an equivalence test; it now uses the equivalence check.
- `lagrange_inversion` divided `w / f` before differentiating, which is `0/0` at the expansion point for every `f` except the identity. It now computes the exact series of the inverse function and rejects non-invertible input.
- Forced 2×2 linear systems omitted the Duhamel convolution of the forcing term.
- The symbolic tier labelled a tangent-line approximation as order 3; it is a first-order expansion with an order-2 remainder.
- `DysonSeries::first_order_term` returned the integrand when the integral had no closed form.
- `sum k^2` returned `k^2 * n` (any non-symbol summand was treated as constant).
- `y' = 1` returned an expression containing `1 / 0`.
- The Fibonacci recurrence returned `0`.
- Caputo derivative used the wrong kernel exponent for orders other than n - 1/2.
- `solve_pde_heat_1d` returned `cos(k_x)` with no `x` and a decay rate of `k_x` instead of `k_x^2`.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-17

### Added
- Overhaul (`23f508b`): `cat-core` 4-fidelity evaluation, BSGS tensor canonicalisation, generalised transforms and a polyglot DSL.
- CAS depth overhaul with multivalued and uncertainty support, and an audit regression suite (`4c2a293`).

- Limits at +infinity by dominant-term analysis (radicals, variable exponents); two-sided limits through `abs`, `sign`, `floor` and `ceil` compare the one-sided values.
- Factoring of a difference of squares with multi-term roots (`a^2 + 2ab + b^2 - c^2`), using an exact multivariate polynomial square root.
- The simplifier merges repeated bases (`x * x` is `x^2`) and combines square roots of positive rationals (`sqrt(2) * sqrt(8)` is `4`).

### Changed
- All three formatters (LaTeX, Unicode, MathML) print with operator precedence: parentheses where needed, negative terms as subtraction. Output text changes, so anything matching printed strings must be updated.
- `ROADMAP.md` marks phases 8, 28 and 30 as Partial or Foundation, with what the code does.

### Fixed
- `factor_primes` printed a composite that Pollard rho could not split as a prime; it is now marked `[composite, not factored]`.
- `exp(2*x) = 7` returned `ln(-sqrt(7))` as a root; roots of equations quadratic in `e^x` print as `ln(7)/2` and `ln(7)/2 + i*pi`.
